# 診病筆記（慶應義塾大學 F-シ-191，正文記 1837 年） / Shinbyō hikki [Diagnostic notes and case records]: Transcription Dataset

《診病筆記》（Shinbyō hikki）是三宅均記錄的漢文診察手稿，開頭追記天保丁酉（1837）的疫熱，後列個別病人、年歲及病程。可用於研究江戶末期醫者的病情觀察及其記錄方式。底本為慶應義塾大學北里紀念醫學圖書館 F-シ-191。

Shinbyō hikki is a Literary Chinese diagnostic notebook attributed to Miyake (三宅均). It opens with dated observations from 1837 and continues with individual cases, patient ages, and courses of illness. The edition follows the manuscript F-シ-191 at Keio University’s Kitasato Memorial Medical Library.

## V0.1.0

同裝冊 26 幅全部定位。轉錄第5幅左面至第11幅左面的漢文正篇（7 幅、13 個有字書面），另錄封籤及前葉署款。第12幅起為附稿，保留影像入口。本版為 AI 輔助逐欄初錄，筆形未明處留疑。

All 26 scans are indexed. The Literary Chinese work occupies 13 written faces across scans 5–11. Cover labels and the prefatory signature are recorded separately. Subsequent bound-in writings are linked at their source locations. AI-assisted initial transcription with unresolved handwriting marked.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。正文與眉上或跨欄的小字分錄。篇前署三宅均，後附詩稿另署三宅春齡，原件及館目未說明兩名關係。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Substantive marginal text is identified separately. The prefatory inscription and appended poetry carry different signatures; their relationship is unverified. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
